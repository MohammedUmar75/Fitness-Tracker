Transform your health journey with an all-in-one fitness and wellness companion built to help you reach your daily goals. Whether you're aiming to lose weight, build muscle, or maintain a healthy lifestyle, this app provides real-time activity tracking, comprehensive nutrition logging, and personalized wellness analytics. Track your daily steps, active burn, macro targets, and hydration level effortlessly while keeping tabs on your health profile with built-in BMI, BMR, and TDEE calculators. With sleek dynamic trends and customizable daily goals, taking charge of your fitness has never been more intuitive.
✨ Key Features at a Glance
Daily Step & Activity Tracker: Real-time step counter, distance tracking, and active calorie burn monitoring.
Nutrition & Macro Logger: Log meals, track daily caloric intake, and customize target protein, carb, and fat goals.
Hydration Counter: Log daily water intake to ensure optimal hydration throughout the day.
Health & Body Calculators: Instant computations for Body Mass Index (BMI), Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR), Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE), and Ideal Weight.
Weight & Progress Trends: Dynamic history logs to monitor weight changes and wellness metrics over time.
